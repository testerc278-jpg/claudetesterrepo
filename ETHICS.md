# Acceptable Use & Ethics Policy

OpenProfile is a business-intelligence (OSINT) tool. It profiles **businesses as legal
entities**, not private individuals.

## Hard rules enforced by the tool

1. **Public data only.** No authenticated scraping, no circumvention of access controls.
2. **Respect source terms.** The tool honours `robots.txt`, applies rate limiting, sends a
   descriptive User-Agent, and prefers official APIs/open data over scraping.
3. **Provenance on every field.** Each datum records its source URL and retrieval time.
4. **Confidence on every inference.** Classifications and the trading score are presented
   with calibrated confidence, never as established fact.
5. **No personal-data profiling.** Sole traders blur the business/person line; the tool does
   not attempt to enrich, locate, or profile the individuals behind an entity.

## Out of scope

- Surveillance of individuals, adverse-action decisions made solely on tool output,
  or any use prohibited by the data sources' terms of service.

Outputs are estimates to support human analysts. A human must review low-confidence results
before any decision is made.
